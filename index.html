Features

Bombu features

CDJ-inspired detail. Built for precise, hands-on performance.

01SMART LOOPING
↔

Stay inside the groove.

Set beat-aware loops, halve or double their length, and move them with Beat Jump. Fine-tune the in/out points with the jog, then save the loop for later.

02PRECISION TRIM

Set the level. Keep the detail.

Adjust each deck’s TRIM from −12 to +12 dB on top of automatic loudness matching. Smooth gain changes give you precise control, with output peak protection kept separate.

03SMART SAMPLE

Make the phrase your instrument.

Hold CUE to choose an instrumental phrase, then tap any pad to play its short accents in order. Shape a new rhythm from the music already in your hands.

04STEM CONTROL

A gesture. A different track.

Hold a deck’s Shift button and move the crossfader. Release Shift to choose the instrumental, vocals or the full track—without changing the channel mix level.
